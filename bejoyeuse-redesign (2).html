About Us

Faith. French. Feminine.

BeJoyeuse began in 2017 selling hand-woven scarves in support of missions abroad. The name — French for "joyous," feminine form — evolved alongside the brand itself. In 2025 we pivoted to handcrafted shea + tallow body butter, magnesium soaks, and beeswax candles, built on the same foundation of faith from Psalms 30:11–12.

An act of joy, even in mourning. That's BeJoyeuse.
